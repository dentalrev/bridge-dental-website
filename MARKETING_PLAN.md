# Bridge Dental — 90-Day Marketing Plan

**Budget: $2,500 · Goal: Fill the 10-seat Founding Cohort at $199/mo**

This plan is written to be followed like a checklist. Do the items in order. Each week has 3–5 concrete actions. Everything assumes what the site already says: early access, Open Dental focus, Medicaid + commercial verification, founding price $199/mo (400 verifications included).

---

## 1. The one-line strategy

> Sell 10 founding seats through founder-led outreach to Open Dental offices (starting with Arkansas Medicaid practices), and use a small paid budget only to capture people already searching for this exact problem.

At $2,500 you cannot out-spend anyone. But this is a niche product with an identifiable buyer (Open Dental offices, office managers drowning in payer portals), so **direct outreach + community presence beats ads**. Ads are a supplement, not the engine.

**Who you're selling to (in priority order):**
1. Arkansas dental offices on Open Dental that see Medicaid patients (your Medicaid MCO feature is a wedge nobody else has locally)
2. Any Open Dental office with 1–5 locations and a busy front desk
3. Office managers in dental admin communities (they feel the pain daily and influence the buying decision)

**The pitch, in the buyer's words:** "Your front desk gets 30–60 minutes a day back. Two clicks runs Medicaid verification for the whole day's schedule and writes it back into Open Dental."

Founder time (free) is the biggest line item that isn't on this table: **budget 5–8 hours/week** for outreach, demos, and community participation.

## 3. One-time setup (Week 1 — before spending anything)

- [ ] **Add a "Book a demo" link (Calendly) to the site and every email.** Right now the only conversion path is a form → email. A calendar link removes a whole back-and-forth. Put it on the early-access page and in the nav.
- [ ] **Confirm the FormSubmit relay is activated** (the first submission to `admin@bridgedental.ai` requires a one-time confirmation click). Send a test lead through `earlyaccess.html` and verify it arrives.
- [ ] **Install analytics** (Google Analytics 4 or Plausible) so you know which channel produced each form fill. Add UTM tags to every link you post (e.g. `?utm_source=fbgroup`).
- [ ] **Set up a lead tracker** — HubSpot free CRM or even a spreadsheet with columns: practice, contact, source, stage (contacted / demo booked / demo done / closed), next step, date.
- [ ] **Record the demo video** (2–3 minutes, screen recording): day's schedule → "Run all Medicaid checks" → results imported into Open Dental. This becomes your #1 sales asset — link it everywhere.
- [ ] **Write the one case study.** You already have a pilot quote ("What used to take me hours now takes just minutes"). Turn it into a half-page story: office size, before-state, time saved, one number. Get permission to use the office's name if possible; "a 2-location Open Dental practice in Arkansas" works if not.
- [ ] **Add urgency honestly:** the pricing page already says "10 spots left." Keep that number accurate and update it publicly as seats fill — it's your best closing tool.

## 4. The 90-day playbook

### Month 1 — Outbound + foundations (target: 25 conversations, 8 demos, 1–2 closed)

**Week 1** (setup week — see checklist above), plus:
- [ ] Build a list of 100 target practices. Sources: Arkansas Medicaid dental provider directory (public), Google Maps "dentist" searches in AR metros, Apollo.io filtered to dental practices. Prioritize ones you can confirm or reasonably guess use Open Dental.
- [ ] Join communities (as yourself, a practicing dentist — not as a brand): Open Dental Users group on Facebook, Dental Office Managers community groups, Dentaltown forums. **Do not pitch yet.** Answer questions for two weeks first.

**Week 2**
- [ ] Send 25 outreach emails/calls. Script: *"I'm a dentist in [state]. My front desk was losing an hour a day to payer portals, so we built a tool that runs the whole day's Medicaid verifications in two clicks and writes them back into Open Dental. We're onboarding 10 founding offices at $199/mo locked for life. Worth a 15-minute look?"* + demo video link + Calendly link.
- [ ] Call (don't just email) the 10 best-fit Arkansas Medicaid practices. Front desk picks up; ask for the office manager. Offer to drop by.

**Week 3**
- [ ] Second batch of 25 outreach touches; follow up everyone from Week 2 (one bump email: "any interest?" — most replies come from the follow-up, not the first email).
- [ ] Launch Google Ads: search-only campaign, ~$10/day. Exact/phrase match keywords: "dental insurance verification software", "open dental insurance verification", "dental eligibility verification software", "medicaid dental verification". Send clicks to `earlyaccess.html`. Add negative keywords ("jobs", "training", "course") from day one.
- [ ] Post your first value post in one community: not a pitch — a "here's how we cut verification time at my own office" story with a screenshot. Mention early access only if people ask or rules allow.

**Week 4**
- [ ] Review: which source produced demos? Double down there in Month 2.
- [ ] Close attempts: every completed demo gets a same-day follow-up with the founding offer + "X spots left."
- [ ] Book the local event/sponsorship (study club, Arkansas State Dental Association meeting, or an Open Dental regional users meetup) for Month 2–3, or trigger the direct-mail postcard batch instead.

### Month 2 — Community + local push (target: 40 conversations, 10 demos, 2–4 closed)

- [ ] Keep the outbound rhythm: 25 new touches + all follow-ups every week. This is the engine; protect the time.
- [ ] Publish the case study on the site and share it in communities and outreach emails.
- [ ] Run the local event or drop-in visits. Bring a laptop with the live demo. Goal: 15 conversations, 5 demos booked.
- [ ] Announce the referral offer to your first customers: refer another office that joins, get a month free.
- [ ] Ads: kill keywords with clicks but no form fills; shift budget to the ones producing leads.
- [ ] Ask your first paying offices for a named testimonial and a Google review of Bridge Dental.

### Month 3 — Close the cohort (target: fill remaining seats)

- [ ] Countdown messaging: "3 founding seats left at $199 — price goes to $499 at launch." Post it, email it to every open lead, put it on the site banner.
- [ ] Re-contact every demo that didn't close ("we're closing the cohort — in or out?"). A clean deadline closes stalled deals.
- [ ] Deploy the $250 contingency into whichever channel produced customers (more ads, another mail batch, or a second event).
- [ ] Start a simple waitlist for the $499 launch tier so momentum carries past the cohort.

## 5. Weekly scorecard (10 minutes every Friday)

Track only these five numbers:

| Metric | Weekly target |
|---|---|
| New outreach touches (email/call/DM) | 25 |
| Conversations started (replies) | 5+ |
| Demos completed | 2–3 |
| Founding seats closed (cumulative) | on pace for 10 by day 90 |
| Cost per demo (ad spend ÷ ad-sourced demos) | under $75 |

If demos-per-week is on target, everything else follows. If it isn't, the fix is almost always "more outreach touches," not "more ad spend."

## 6. Income projections (Months 1–3)

Assumptions: founding price **$199/mo per location**, ~10–15% of contacted practices take a demo, ~25–35% of demos convert, 2–6 week sales cycle (so Month 1 closes are few), negligible overage revenue early, no churn inside 90 days. "Income" = MRR billed that month.

| | Conservative | **Base case** | Optimistic |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Month 1** — customers (end of month) | 1 | **2** | 3 |
| Month 1 income | $199 | **$398** | $597 |
| **Month 2** — customers | 3 | **5** | 7 |
| Month 2 income | $597 | **$995** | $1,393 |
| **Month 3** — customers | 5 | **8** | 10 (cohort full) |
| Month 3 income | $995 | **$1,592** | $1,990 |
| **3-month total collected** | $1,791 | **$2,985** | $3,980 |

Read on the numbers:
- **Base case pays back the $2,500 budget within the first 90 days** ($2,985 collected), and the real prize is the run-rate: 8 founding customers = **$19,100/year** of recurring revenue locked in, plus a proven playbook before raising the price to $499.
- The conservative case ($1,791 collected) still leaves ~$12k/yr of MRR — the budget pays back in month 4–5.
- These are *founding-cohort* numbers. The moment the cohort fills, the same funnel sells $499/mo seats, and month-4+ income scales accordingly.

**Biggest risk to the projection:** slow sales cycles, not lack of interest. Dental offices decide slowly. The mitigations are built into the plan: calls instead of just emails, live local demos, a real deadline ("cohort closes"), and the price-lock incentive.

---

## 7. Rules of thumb

1. **Outreach is the engine; ads are a net.** If you skip a week of outreach, no amount of ad spend recovers it.
2. **Sell the time saved, not the software.** "30–60 minutes a day back" closes; feature lists don't.
3. **The Medicaid two-click demo is the wow moment.** Lead every demo with it.
4. **Follow up twice.** Most closes come from touch #2 or #3.
5. **Keep the "X spots left" number honest and public.** Scarcity only works while it's true.
