Questions

Everything about the Private Release

What exactly does "Private Release" mean?
Ten practices, invited personally, before the public release. You get the product at a permanently reduced price; we get honest feedback from real practices that directly shapes what we build. It's a trade of early trust for locked-in pricing — not a discount promotion.
When am I actually billed?
Never today. Your card reserves the spot, your first month is free, and Private Release billing at $39 per location per month begins automatically after the free month. You'll know the exact date at checkout.
Can I cancel?
Yes. Cancel any time before the first paid month and you pay nothing. After that there's still no contract — monthly billing, cancel anytime.
Does it work with my practice software?
Bridge Analytics is built specifically for Open Dental. Other systems aren't supported yet.
How does per-location pricing work?
Pricing is per location — each office pays the full monthly price and gets its own complete dashboard, goals, and team access. Unlimited standard users are included at every location.
What does onboarding look like?
After you reserve, we schedule an onboarding call: we connect your Open Dental data, configure your goals, add your users, and verify your core metrics with you before you rely on them.
Where do the numbers and benchmarks come from?
Your practice metrics come straight from your Open Dental data, with drill-downs that reconcile against headline totals. Local market comparisons — competitor standings, review velocity, local-pack rankings — are live today in Bridge Reputation and use only verified public Google data. Operational benchmarks will use aggregated, de-identified data from the Bridge network — and until a benchmark is ready and defensible, we label it "coming soon" or don't show it.
Is Bridge Reputation really included?
Yes. Bridge Reputation — an AI draft for every review, automated review requests, local competitor rank tracking, patient-voice themes, and the content studio — is part of the platform, and your locked $39 per location per month covers all of it. No add-on fee, during the free month or after.
What's expected of me as a Private Release practice?
Use it in your real daily operations and tell us the truth about it for the first 90 days — what's working, what's confusing, what's missing. That feedback is the whole point of the Private Release.
What happens after the 10 spots are gone?
The next group joins at a higher Early Access price — still below the eventual public price, but without the permanent $39 lock. The 10 Private Release spots are the best terms Bridge Analytics will ever be offered on.
