Fair questions

Who actually writes the review replies?+
Bridge drafts every reply in your practice's voice the moment a review syncs. A person on your team reads it, tweaks a word if they want, and posts — a HIPAA guardrail checks every edit, so a reply can never confirm someone is a patient or mention treatment.
Do posts really publish themselves?+
Yes. A guided wizard connects your Facebook, Instagram and Google Business accounts — no developer needed. Bridge pre-fills a calendar of on-brand posts; you approve, and they go out on schedule. Nothing ever publishes without your standing approval, and comments from every channel land in one inbox.
Where do the competitor numbers come from?+
Public Google data only — ratings, review counts, review pace, local-pack positions — snapshotted over time. When there isn't enough history for an honest number, you see a dash, never a made-up zero. No invented estimates, no claims about anyone's patients.

Bridge is built for Open Dental practices. Reputation connects to your Google Business Profile and social accounts, and times review requests off your Open Dental schedule — they go out the moment the appointment ends, while the visit is still fresh.
